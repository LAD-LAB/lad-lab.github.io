# Long-Read Sequencing (PacBio)

Long-read sequencing has certain advantages over our in-lab 16S sequencing with Illumina, like its high accuracy and species-level resolution, and the lab plans to switch to long-read sequencing in the future with the help of the Duke Microbiome Core. However, one of the drawbacks is that the pipeline takes forever; the one cohort I've run through so far took about five days to complete.
