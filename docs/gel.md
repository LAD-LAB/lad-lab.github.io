# Gel Electrophoresis

After PCR, we run gel electrophoresis to confirm that the target amplicon was successfully amplified before proceeding to cleanup. The lab uses three gel formats depending on throughput:

| Format | Throughput | Run Time | Stain | Cat. No. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agarose gel (1%) | Flexible (8–50 wells) | ~35 min at 80V | GelGreen or GelRed (added manually) | Agarose: Invitrogen 16500 |
| E-Gel 96 (2%, pre-cast) | 96 samples | 12 min | SYBR Safe (pre-cast) | Invitrogen G720802 |
| E-Gel 48 (2%, pre-cast) | 48 samples | 20 min | SYBR Safe (pre-cast) | Invitrogen G720802 |

For routine FoodSeq or 16S runs with a full plate, the E-Gel 96 is the fastest option. For smaller batches or when you need more control over gel conditions, a standard agarose gel works well. All three formats are standard protocols; follow the manufacturer's instructions for gel preparation, loading, and running.

We image gels on the Licor imaging system (for E-Gels) or a UV transilluminator / gel doc system (for agarose gels).

With amplification verified, proceed to [Amplicon Cleaning and Quantification](cleaning.md).
