Megaphyloseq PCA Explorer

Drop a phyloseq .Rds file to compute and explore its PCA. Hover for sample details; click cohort chips to highlight.

Interpretation notes
  • This is an exploratory visualization, not a statistical test. Apparent clustering may not be significant.
  • PCA axes capture the directions of greatest variance, which may reflect batch effects, read depth, or dominant taxa rather than biology of interest.
  • Unlocked (live) mode uses an approximate PCA (power iteration). Scores and variance explained may differ slightly from exact results in R. Use locked mode for reporting.
  • Samples with low read counts can appear as outliers. Use the min reads filter to assess their influence.
  • Controls and human reads are removed before analysis. Read counts shown are post-filter.
