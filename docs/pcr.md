# PCR Amplification

The FoodSeq workflow uses a two-step PCR approach: a primary qPCR amplifies the target locus (trnL for plants, 12SV5 for animals) from extracted DNA, and an indexing qPCR adds sample-specific barcodes in a second round of amplification.

The singleplex protocols for primary and indexing qPCR are available on GitHub:

- [**WI-PA003** — Primary qPCR for trnLGH](https://github.com/LAD-LAB/foodseq-protocols/blob/main/WI-PA003.md)
- [**WI-PA003B** — Primary qPCR for 12SV5](https://github.com/LAD-LAB/foodseq-protocols/blob/main/WI-PA003b.md)
- [**WI-PA004** — Indexing qPCR](https://github.com/LAD-LAB/foodseq-protocols/blob/main/WI-PA004.md)

After PCR amplification, verify your products by [Gel Electrophoresis](gel.md).
